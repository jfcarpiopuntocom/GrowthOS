# SSS Trust Guarantee

## EN — The SSS Trust Guarantee

If you pay for SSS Pro, you are buying a transparent diagnostic tool — not a black box and not a promise that your company will survive.

We guarantee that:
- the score is generated from the published 8-metric method;
- the scoring rules and weights are inspectable in the open-source engine;
- the same valid inputs produce the same score;
- SSS does not require a bank login, transaction feed, accounting connection or financial document upload;
- SSS application code does not intentionally persist the eight scoring inputs;
- Pro features described at purchase will be available to a valid Pro licence.

If SSS Pro materially fails to provide the described Pro functionality, the commercial refund policy should make the buyer whole. Final refund window and payment-processor wording should be set when checkout is implemented.

SSS does not guarantee business survival, solvency, fundraising, profitability or future outcomes.
