# SSS — World Distribution & Growth System

## Product truth

Do not sell "eight metrics."
Sell **time to decide**.

Core promise:
> Eight coarse business numbers → one survival score → three things to fix first.

Narrative hook:
> When the goal keeps moving away, measure what is shortening your time to act.

The diagnostic must feel useful before the user ever sees a commercial path.

## The flywheel

1. **Problem intent**
   - "my business is growing but cash is tighter"
   - "how do I avoid closing"
   - "growth pains"
   - "cash crunch"
   - "am I ready to scale"
2. **Instant SSS diagnosis**
3. **Shareable scorecard**
4. **Return next month / compare trend**
5. **Deeper operational tool when relevant**
   - inventory / working-capital risk → Stock Semaphore
6. **External Survival Suite entitlement**
7. **Advisor / accelerator distribution**
8. **Anonymized, opt-in aggregate insights → better content and discovery**

## What we take from the Pre-Mortem analyses

### Adopt

- Sell "time to decide," not dashboard complexity.
- Use real founder language and real situations.
- Make the first output immediately actionable.
- Build a recurring monthly habit.
- Create shareable scorecards for partner / accountant / advisor conversations.
- Build distribution channels that do not depend on founder heroics.
- Create vertical entry pages and pain-language SEO/AEO.
- Use advisor/accountant/accelerator channels.
- Treat free diagnosis as the relationship opener, not a crippled demo.
- Build a public category around early-stage survival / the 6–24 month valley while also serving restructured businesses.

### Modify for ChatGPT

- No forced email gate before receiving the diagnosis.
- No aggressive upsell inside the plugin.
- No requirement to identify the business.
- No bank/accounting/document connections.
- Optional external continuity after value is delivered.

### Reject

- Any claim that SSS predicts failure with certainty.
- Any dependence on invented social proof or fabricated "cases."
- Any marketing statistic without a traceable source.
- Any funnel mechanic that contradicts the privacy promise.

## Content system

Create source-backed pages and short-form content from the same matrix:

**Pain → number → decision → tool**

Examples:
- Revenue is up, cash is down → working capital / burn multiple → diagnose → SSS
- Great sales, one giant customer → concentration risk → diagnose → SSS
- Fast growth, expensive acquisition → CAC payback → diagnose → SSS
- Inventory eating cash → working capital → diagnose → Stock Semaphore
- Founder cannot leave for a week → operational dependency → future module / Survival Suite

The content should be useful even without installing the plugin.

## Monthly habit

Free:
- current SSS
- top 3 risks
- current action plan

Pro entitlement:
- monthly history
- score delta
- weakest-metric trend
- 24-month stress scenarios
- executive report
- cross-tool workflows

The retention hook is not "come back because we emailed you."
It is:
> **Is my survival score improving?**

## Suite architecture

### Startup Survival Score
Macro financial survival diagnosis.

### Stock Semaphore
Inventory and working-capital diagnosis.

Bridge rule:
If SSS flags working-capital stress and the user holds inventory, recommend Stock Semaphore as the next diagnostic step.

Never recommend Stock Semaphore merely because it is another J.F. Carpio product.

## 60-content engine

Do not create 60 unrelated videos.
Create 6 series × 10:

1. "When growth makes cash worse"
2. "The number founders discover too late"
3. "This business did not die because..."
4. "One minute survival check"
5. "Growth pain or fatal signal?"
6. "What your accountant sees that you do not"

Each piece maps to exactly one discovery intent and one diagnostic action.
