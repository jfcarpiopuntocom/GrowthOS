# Business Survival Score — Privacy Policy

Updated: 2026-10-02

Business Survival Score is provided by the JFCarpio.com team. This policy covers the website at https://business-survival-score.jfcarpio.com and the Business Survival Score plugin for ChatGPT.

## 1. Data we collect

Only what you type or import: eight aggregate business metrics (cash runway, CAC payback, burn multiple, gross margin, working capital ratio, largest customer share, operating cash flow to debt service, capital efficiency) and your language choice (EN, ES or PT).

We do not ask for your name, email, account, bank access, credentials, customer records or payment details, and we do not use cookies, advertising trackers or analytics.

## 2. How we use it

Solely to calculate and display your score and the metrics to review first. We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, selling, or training models.

## 3. Who receives it

On the website, your metrics never leave your browser. In ChatGPT, the metrics pass through OpenAI to our calculation server, hosted by Cloudflare. As with any website, Cloudflare and OpenAI handle network metadata such as your IP address and request time to deliver the service under their own privacy policies; we do not collect or keep it. We share data with no one else.

## 4. How long we keep it

We keep nothing. The server calculates the result in memory during the request and discards it; there is no database and no application logging of your metrics. In the browser, reloading the page clears everything. Your ChatGPT conversation may keep the tool inputs and results according to your OpenAI settings.

## 5. Your controls

You choose what to enter and can leave at any time. Use Export to save your own copy as a file on your device. Delete or manage ChatGPT conversations in your OpenAI settings. Because we store nothing, there is nothing for us to delete.

Questions and issue reports: https://github.com/jfcarpiopuntocom/GrowthOS/issues

## 6. Changes

We will update the date on this page when this policy changes.
